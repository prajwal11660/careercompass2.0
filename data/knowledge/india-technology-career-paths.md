# Technology career paths and skill evidence

Reference: [National Qualifications Register (India)](https://www.nqr.gov.in/)

This project-authored guide summarizes common skill areas; it is not an official qualification standard. Use the National Qualifications Register and current employer job descriptions to check exact Indian qualification and role requirements.

## Software development

Common foundations include one programming language, data structures and problem solving, version control, testing, debugging, and communicating implementation trade-offs. Project evidence should describe what the person built, their contribution, and a verifiable result.

## Cloud and platform engineering

Common foundations include Linux and networking basics, scripting, cloud concepts, identity and access management, deployment automation, monitoring, and reliability. Progression is stronger when candidates show a small deployed project, safe configuration, logs or metrics, and an explanation of cost and failure handling.

## Cybersecurity

Common foundations include networking, operating-system basics, identity, threat awareness, secure configuration, and incident documentation. Recommend legal labs and defensive projects; never imply that a certificate alone proves practical competence.

## Responsible role recommendations

These skill groups are indicative rather than mandatory. Requirements change by employer and level. Distinguish skills evidenced in a resume from skills to learn next, and direct users to current official qualification records and job postings before making career decisions.
