# Data and business analytics career paths

Reference: [National Career Service (India)](https://www.ncs.gov.in/)

This project-authored guide is a starting point, not an official job standard. Employers use different titles and requirements; use the National Career Service and current job descriptions to verify local role names, qualifications, and openings.

## Common role families

- **Data analyst:** turns data into answers to business questions. Common evidence includes SQL queries, spreadsheet analysis, data cleaning, basic statistics, and clearly explained findings.
- **Business intelligence analyst:** builds recurring reports and dashboards. Common evidence includes a BI tool, data modelling basics, metric definitions, and communicating trends to non-technical colleagues.
- **Junior data engineer:** prepares reliable datasets for analysis. Common evidence includes SQL, Python, data pipelines, APIs or cloud storage, and testing or monitoring.
- **Business or operations analyst:** translates a process or business problem into measurable requirements. Common evidence includes process analysis, stakeholder interviews, documentation, spreadsheets, and outcome measurement.

## Interpreting fit

Treat these as overlapping role families rather than guaranteed job matches. Recommend a role only when the resume contains relevant evidence. Explain which evidence transfers and which skills are still missing. Do not infer a qualification, work authorization, salary, or local hiring requirement from a role title alone.

For an India-specific qualification or occupational standard, search the National Career Service and National Qualifications Register. Requirements vary by employer, state, experience level, and sector.
