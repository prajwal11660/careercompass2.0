# Career roadmaps and evidence-based resume advice

Reference: [National Career Service (India)](https://www.ncs.gov.in/)

This project-authored framework is general coaching advice, not an official employment guarantee. Adapt the plan to the user's goals, experience, time, and current local job descriptions.

## A practical 30/60/90-day learning plan

- **Days 1-30 — focus:** choose one target role, compare several current job descriptions, identify two or three recurring skill gaps, and learn one foundational concept at a time. Define a small portfolio deliverable.
- **Days 31-60 — practice:** complete a project that demonstrates the target skills with a README, clear decisions, and a reproducible result. Ask a peer or mentor for feedback and improve the work.
- **Days 61-90 — demonstrate and apply:** polish the project and resume, practice explaining decisions, request feedback, and apply selectively to roles that match demonstrated evidence. Review outcomes and adjust the plan.

The number of weeks is a planning aid, not a promise of employability. Recommend a realistic workload and prioritize fundamentals over collecting unrelated certificates.

## Resume evidence

- Prefer concise, specific bullets describing action, tools, scope, and result. Quantify an outcome only when the person can support the number.
- Separate skills used in a project from skills merely studied.
- Tailor the summary and project order to the role, but do not invent experience or copy job-posting claims that are unsupported.
- Encourage proofreading, readable headings, and checking that links to projects work.
- Do not repeat or expose personal contact details in generated advice unless the user asks about them.

## India-specific job-search safety

The National Career Service states its services are free and warns jobseekers about fraudulent claims and fees. Advise users to verify opportunities through official channels and not pay a recruiter or employer for registration, interviews, or job offers. Check the current NCS guidance directly because policies and warnings may change.
